About me

I'm a data professional with a BSc Honours in Data Science and Systems and hands-on experience keeping operational data accurate, organised and on time. At Magaya Mining I lead the migration of laboratory records into a new management system and track samples from reception to reporting.

My toolkit is Python, SQL, R, Power Query and Power BI, backed by solid statistics and machine learning. I enjoy the unglamorous parts of data work that make everything else possible: clean inputs, validation, and workflows that run without surprises.

Beyond pipelines I'm drawn to AI, especially deep learning and computer vision, and I build projects in those areas too. I'm looking for a data engineering role where I can grow into larger-scale pipelines and collaborate on projects that matter.
